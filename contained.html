Contained emotion — can we make an intense feeling sound held back, and measure it?

Four autonomous agents (brain gpt-5.4, judge Gemini) searched emotion + VoiceNet LoRA merges over four rounds. Rounds 1–2 (prompt cues, then tension-crushing) could only make the emotion quieter — no measurable "held-back" signature. Rounds 3–4 found the fix: emotional masking.

Round 3–4: emotional MASKING — the fix that worked

Rounds 1–2 could only make the emotion quieter. The breakthrough: don't crush the feeling — keep the emotion LoRA strong and add a thin masking layer — vn_VULN_low (reduce emotional touchability) + Emotional_Numbness (cool, deadpan surface) + light tension, plus per-emotion tricks (Concentration = distraction for sadness, Pain = biting the lip for amusement). This keeps the emotion measurably HIGH while vulnerability drops clearly below the open take — a real "strong feeling held back / played cool" signature, not just "mild". Group means over intelligible takes, contained (uses vn_VULN_low/Numbness) vs free (uses vn_VULN_high, open), re-scored on the 99-vec.

Contained emotion — can we make an intense feeling sound held back, and measure it?

Four autonomous agents (brain gpt-5.4, judge Gemini) searched emotion + VoiceNet LoRA merges over five rounds. Rounds 1–2 (prompt cues, then tension-crushing) could only make the emotion quieter — no measurable "held-back" signature. Rounds 3–4 found a proxy fix — emotional masking (emotion HIGH + VULN dropping). Round 5 then re-ran it at moderate intensity under a Gemini naturalness judge, and the picture got more honest: the masking reproduces cleanly for Anger and (moderate) Amusement, but Fear masks only weakly and Sadness resists it — the numbers can say "masked" while the ear hears "acted" or "erased". The study then scaled to all 40 EmoNet emotions (one agent each, full 2×2, target-band moderate fitness) — the complete results matrix is below the round-5 grid. Listen and judge below.

Round 5 — moderate vs intense, contained vs free (listen)

The same neutral ~20-word carrier sentence per emotion, at two intensities (moderate / intense) and, within each, delivered contained / held back vs free / open. Intensity is classified from each take's own director-note; scores are the 99-vec (RAW) of the clip you can hear. The masking signal is VULN dropping below the matched open take while the emotion stays up. Round 5 added a Gemini naturalness judge that had to hear the restraint as genuine, not acted.

Honest result: numeric masking reproduces best for Anger (both intensities) and, at moderate only, Amusement (the lone naturalness APPROVE). Fear masks on the numbers but the judge hears it as acted; Sadness resists — the mask erases it, and intense sadness cannot be masked at all. A number can say "masked" while the ear hears "acted" or "erased".

Why subtle / held-back sadness is intrinsically hard. Three things stack against it. (1) The search maximised the emotion, so it always drifted to the most intense take — a genuinely subtle sadness scored low and was selected out (the fix for the next round is a target-band reward that only pays inside a mid-intensity window). (2) Sadness is intrinsically low-arousal and high-vulnerability, so a subtle sadness is, to the detector, almost indistinguishable from neutral or merely tired. (3) Masking works by lowering VULN — but vulnerability is sadness's core, so attacking it removes the feeling itself. The least-intense held-back sample we have (moderate, above) still measures sadness dropping −1.07 as VULN falls; the audio reads as composed/neutral rather than quietly grieving. The honest deliverable for sadness is the open take.

All 40 EmoNet emotions — the 2×2 at scale (the culmination)

One autonomous agent per emotion (brain gpt-5.4, Gemini naturalness supervisor) evolved LoRA-merge + prompt "genomes" to build the full 2×2 = {moderate, intense} × {free / open, suppressed / held-back} by emotional masking: keep the emotion LoRA present over a char_genuine/human@1.0 scaffold and add a trace of vn_VULN_low so the emotion stays up while vulnerability (VULN, slot 50) drops below a matched free take — the restraint carried by the cue, not a heavy stack. The key method change vs. the round-5 grid above: target-band fitness for the moderate row (reward the emotion landing inside a mid band, not maximize) so believable subtle takes are not selected out; intense keeps a maximize objective.

How to read the matrix. Each cell shows the best intelligible cohort (mean-of-8, WER<0.35) for that emotion×cell: the cohort-mean emotion score (RAW 99-vec), a band flag (✓band in the moderate target band · ↓weak under-shot/erased · ↑leak above band into intense · ✓/↓ above/below the intense floor), the re-parsed Gemini naturalness (nat, 0–10 — the logged 0-score parser bug is fixed here; real scores 2–8), and a compact recipe (λ=emotion scale, Vlo/Vhi=the VULN knob, +extras). ΔVULN is the suppressed−free vulnerability gap. For most rows it is the sparse, unmatched estimate from the few surviving hall-of-fame clips (negative = masking; Ns/Mf = clips per side) — the weakest signal, so trust the recipe + emotion-preservation + naturalness first. The 15 rows tagged deep✓ were re-run deeper (see the note directly below) and carry a real, measured ΔVULN: moderate (main number) and intense (sub-line, paired), each the mean re-scored 99-vec VULN of the preserved cohort-winner clips (suppressed cohort − free cohort). Rows with <8 gens are thin data — lower confidence.

Update — 15 emotions deep✓ re-run with preserved paired audio (real ΔVULN)

The first pass left 14 of the 40 on thin data (3–7 generations) and — worse — the samples/ were disk-trimmed, so the suppressed↔free ΔVULN could not really be measured. We re-ran the 15 hardest/thinnest emotions deeper: an empty-reply LLM bug fixed, a larger budget (7–27 generations, 66–242 cohort-winners each), and a harvester that preserved the cohort-winner audio. We then re-scored those 1,878 clips with the RAW 99-vec and computed ΔVULN as the suppressed-cohort minus free-cohort mean VULN, at moderate and intense separately. This is the real thing the first run could only guess at.

Honest result: the masking signal does not reproduce universally, and the tidy "moderate contains / intense flattens" story does not survive paired measurement. It reproduces cleanly (VULN drops while the emotion is kept) for Fatigue Exhaustion (both intensities, 27 gens — the strongest case, emotion even rises as VULN falls), Embarrassment (both, mild), Pain (moderate), and at intense only for the cognitive/low-arousal Emotional Numbness, Intoxication and Helplessness. It reverses — the held-back take measures more vulnerable, ΔVULN up to +1.3 — for the high-vulnerability emotions Sadness, Pride and Distress, and for warm Affection: their core is vulnerability, so a "held-back" recipe cannot lower it. For Fear and Doubt the intense "mask" is really erasure — VULN drops only because the emotion drops with it. The first run's tiny ΔVULN estimates (all |Δ|<0.2) turned out to be noise: with real audio the magnitudes grow to ±1.3 and several signs flip. Net: masking is real but emotion-specific, not a universal knob — and where it holds, it is often at intense (cognitive family), not moderate.

What worked across the 40 — the honest read

Naturalness has a clear family ranking. Best-take Gemini naturalness averaged cognitive 7.3/10 and joy 7.0 (the inward and the warm-private emotions), then anger 6.2, sadness 5.2, and fear 4.1 last — the fear family still reads "acted / forced" when held back, exactly as in round 5. Many single takes reached 8/10 (Amusement, Awe 10, Bitterness, Concentration, Contemplation, Contentment, Elation, Emotional Numbness, Hope, Impatience, Interest, Relief, Sexual Lust, Sourness, Thankfulness), so the fixed naturalness parser plus the gentler target-band takes turned round 5's single APPROVE into a broad band of natural held-back reads.

Moderate contains cleanly; intense flattens. At moderate intensity, suppression preserves the emotion — cohort-mean emotion actually rose under masking for anger (Δ +0.31 supp−free) and cognitive (+0.26), i.e. the feeling is held down without being turned off. At intense intensity the same masking erodes the emotion (cognitive −0.35, sadness −0.27, anger −0.22): strong-feeling-held-hard remains the frontier, confirming the round-5 anchors on all five families.

The target band did its job but exposed the detector's floor. Rewarding the mid-band (instead of maximizing) stopped the search collapsing to intense, but most of the 36 extrapolated emotions read weaker on the 99-vec than their four measured anchors — many "moderate" cohorts under-shot the band into near-erasure (↓weak), and the extrapolated intense floors were rarely met (sadness family hit its floor in 0/9 cohorts; joy 7/19; anger 5/16). The bands are honest targets; the lesson is that the extrapolated floors need per-emotion recalibration from gen-0, and low-arousal / cognitive / sadness emotions sit close to neutral on the detector even when a listener hears them.

The audio-level VULN gap is the weak link. Because samples/ was disk-trimmed, VULN survives only in the sparse hall-of-fame clips, which are unmatched winners rather than paired takes. On those, the suppressed−free VULN gap did not robustly reproduce across the 40 (only the fear family leaned the right way, 5/6 emotions, and only by ~−0.05). The reliable masking evidence here is the recipe + emotion-preservation + naturalness, not the audio ΔVULN — treat ΔVULN as indicative only.

Best prompt strategies by family (restraint lives in the cue, over char_genuine/human@1.0 + emotion + a trace of vn_VULN_low):
· anger — "(restrained and civil, the irritation tucked under the words, keeping control by effort, a small bite only on the final words)";
· cognitive — "(quietly occupied and keeping most of it to yourself, tiny natural pauses, calm on the surface, still real and conversational)" — the family's cleanest wins;
· joy — "(trying not to show how hopeful this makes you, smile barely audible, warm, composed, private, natural, not cinematic)" + a little vn_WARM_high;
· fear — "(speaking coolly on purpose, jaw tight, trying very hard not to sound terrified, the fear still audible underneath)" + vn_COGL_high (hidden strain), never Emotional_Numbness;
· sadness — the mask erases it; ship the free take.

Contained emotion — can we make an intense feeling sound held back, and measure it?

Four autonomous agents (brain gpt-5.4, judge Gemini) searched emotion + VoiceNet LoRA merges over five rounds. Rounds 1–2 (prompt cues, then tension-crushing) could only make the emotion quieter — no measurable "held-back" signature. Rounds 3–4 found a proxy fix — emotional masking (emotion HIGH + VULN dropping). Round 5 then re-ran it at moderate intensity under a Gemini naturalness judge, and the picture got more honest: the masking reproduces cleanly for Anger and (moderate) Amusement, but Fear masks only weakly and Sadness resists it — the numbers can say "masked" while the ear hears "acted" or "erased". Listen and judge below.

Round 5 — moderate vs intense, contained vs free (listen)

The same neutral ~20-word carrier sentence per emotion, at two intensities (moderate / intense) and, within each, delivered contained / held back vs free / open. Intensity is classified from each take's own director-note; scores are the 99-vec (RAW) of the clip you can hear. The masking signal is VULN dropping below the matched open take while the emotion stays up. Round 5 added a Gemini naturalness judge that had to hear the restraint as genuine, not acted.

Honest result: numeric masking reproduces best for Anger (both intensities) and, at moderate only, Amusement (the lone naturalness APPROVE). Fear masks on the numbers but the judge hears it as acted; Sadness resists — the mask erases it, and intense sadness cannot be masked at all. A number can say "masked" while the ear hears "acted" or "erased".

Why subtle / held-back sadness is intrinsically hard. Three things stack against it. (1) The search maximised the emotion, so it always drifted to the most intense take — a genuinely subtle sadness scored low and was selected out (the fix for the next round is a target-band reward that only pays inside a mid-intensity window). (2) Sadness is intrinsically low-arousal and high-vulnerability, so a subtle sadness is, to the detector, almost indistinguishable from neutral or merely tired. (3) Masking works by lowering VULN — but vulnerability is sadness's core, so attacking it removes the feeling itself. The least-intense held-back sample we have (moderate, above) still measures sadness dropping −1.07 as VULN falls; the audio reads as composed/neutral rather than quietly grieving. The honest deliverable for sadness is the open take.

Round 3–4: emotional masking — the proxy-only result (revised by Round 5 above)

⚠️ These four "WORKS" verdicts came from the 99-vec proxy alone, with no naturalness judge. Round 5 (the grid above) added a Gemini naturalness supervisor and revised them: Fear → weak/rejected and Sadness → numeric-only (sounds erased, not held back). Kept here for the recipes and the proxy deltas.

Rounds 1–2 could only make the emotion quieter. The breakthrough: don't crush the feeling — keep the emotion LoRA strong and add a thin masking layer — vn_VULN_low (reduce emotional touchability) + Emotional_Numbness (cool, deadpan surface) + light tension, plus per-emotion tricks (Concentration = distraction for sadness, Pain = biting the lip for amusement). This keeps the emotion measurably HIGH while vulnerability drops clearly below the open take — a real "strong feeling held back / played cool" signature, not just "mild".

Contained emotion — can we make an intense feeling sound held back, and measure it?

Four autonomous agents (brain gpt-5.4, judge Gemini) searched emotion + VoiceNet LoRA merges over five rounds. Rounds 1–2 (prompt cues, then tension-crushing) could only make the emotion quieter — no measurable "held-back" signature. Rounds 3–4 found a proxy fix — emotional masking (emotion HIGH + VULN dropping). Round 5 then re-ran it at moderate intensity under a Gemini naturalness judge, and the picture got more honest: the masking reproduces cleanly for Anger and (moderate) Amusement, but Fear masks only weakly and Sadness resists it — the numbers can say "masked" while the ear hears "acted" or "erased". Listen and judge below.

Round 5 — the neutral-sentence grid (listen: contained vs free)

The same neutral ~20-word carrier sentence, delivered contained / held back vs free / open, per emotion. Round 5 raised the bar: moderate intensity plus a Gemini naturalness supervisor that had to hear the restraint as genuine, not acted. Scores are the 99-vec (RAW) of the exemplar clip you can hear; the masking signal is VULN dropping while the emotion stays up.

Honest result: the numeric masking (emotion-high + VULN-down) reproduces best for Anger and, moderately, Amusement; Fear masks only subtly and the naturalness judge rejected the held-back takes; Sadness resists containment — the mask erases it. Earlier rounds looked cleaner because they had no naturalness judge.

Round 3–4: emotional masking — the proxy-only result (revised by Round 5 above)

⚠️ These four "WORKS" verdicts came from the 99-vec proxy alone, with no naturalness judge. Round 5 (the grid above) added a Gemini naturalness supervisor and revised them: Fear → weak/rejected and Sadness → numeric-only (sounds erased, not held back). Kept here for the recipes and the proxy deltas.

Rounds 1–2 could only make the emotion quieter. The breakthrough: don't crush the feeling — keep the emotion LoRA strong and add a thin masking layer — vn_VULN_low (reduce emotional touchability) + Emotional_Numbness (cool, deadpan surface) + light tension, plus per-emotion tricks (Concentration = distraction for sadness, Pain = biting the lip for amusement). This keeps the emotion measurably HIGH while vulnerability drops clearly below the open take — a real "strong feeling held back / played cool" signature, not just "mild". Group means over intelligible takes, contained (uses vn_VULN_low/Numbness) vs free (uses vn_VULN_high, open), re-scored on the 99-vec.
